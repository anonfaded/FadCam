# FadCam Lite Changelog

## 1.0.0
- First release. FadCam's recording engine in a ~14 MB app instead of ~186 MB.
- **Hybrid MP4** recording that survives crashes, power loss, and sudden shutdowns.
- Background recording with the screen off, plus bodycam and dashcam use.
- Front, back, and dual camera where your device supports it.
- Watermarks: timestamp, location, logo, or your own text.
- Video splitting by file size.
- Tab Lock on Home, Records, and Settings with PIN, password, or biometric unlock.
- No ads, no analytics, no trackers, no accounts.
